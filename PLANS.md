# Plans

## Immediate

1. Package the 2,241-edge graph, exact coordinates, CNF, compressed DRAT proof, proper five-colouring, hashes, and one-command verification in a dedicated reproducibility release.
2. Repeat verification from a clean environment and record tool versions and deterministic commands.
3. Complete a careful prior-art review of small and edge-critical 5-chromatic unit-distance graphs before making any novelty or record claim.
4. Test edge-criticality of the 2,241-edge candidate by producing and independently checking a 4-colouring for every single-edge deletion.

## Next searches

1. Reduce the 2,241-edge candidate further with certificate-preserving edge deletion.
2. Explore exact supergraphs produced by rotated copies and Minkowski constructions only after ranking them with reliable CPU checks.
3. Search for constructions with fewer than 509 vertices.
4. Retain CaDiCaL as the sole decision procedure for 4-colourability unless a replacement passes easy-graph sanity tests and real-traffic benchmarks.

## Publication gate

No paper-level or record claim will be made until the reproducibility package passes independent reruns and the prior-art comparison is complete.
